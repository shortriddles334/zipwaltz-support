Delete a Rescue guest profile

Request removal of your Zipwaltz Rescue profile and its online game data from Nimbus Bikes LLC.

With the game installed, open Expedition → Rescue → Rescue privacy & data and confirm the deletion request. The game verifies your profile and gives you a private receipt.

Request deletion by email

You can contact us here even after uninstalling Zipwaltz. Tell us whether you used iPhone or Android. If you already submitted a request, mention that you have a receipt. Support will explain what it needs to verify your request.

Guest profiles are device-based. An email address or a shared room invite alone cannot identify the owner. Your solo save, store purchases, Epic account and profiles in other games are separate from your Zipwaltz Rescue profile.

Check an existing request
