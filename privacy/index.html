Privacy Policy

Zipwaltz · ShortyGames

Effective date: October 14, 2026 · Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains how Zipwaltz handles information in its iOS and Android games and how ShortyGames handles messages sent to its support email and visits to this support website. ShortyGames is the operating name used for Zipwaltz.

1. Playing the game

The game is an arcade game you play on your own device. You do not need to create a game account to play. The game content is bundled in the app, so ordinary gameplay does not require connecting to a ShortyGames server. Duels (section 4) let you send a run to a friend as a link, but they are optional.

The game stores your scores, progress (such as stardust, rank, missions, achievements and cosmetics), Daily Orbit results, run statistics, a preset duel name and settings locally on your device so they can appear in later sessions. ShortyGames does not receive this data through a game backend. Your device, Apple and Google backup settings can affect whether local data is preserved, restored or deleted.

2. Optional purchases

The iOS and Android apps offer optional one-time cosmetic purchases in the in-game Hangar: Pulsar Comets, Rival Ghost Styles, the Strings Instrument Pack, Finish Effects and a Founder's Bundle. They are cosmetic only and do not change gameplay or scoring. On iOS, the original Supporter Pack may also be offered; if you already own it, it keeps working.

Purchases and restoring them (Restore Purchases in the Hangar) are processed by Apple's App Store and StoreKit on iOS, or Google Play Billing on Android. Apple or Google manages billing, your account, transaction processing and purchase history under its own terms and privacy practices, and the app checks the store's verified ownership information to unlock your items. ShortyGames does not operate a payment-processing server for this game and does not receive your payment-card details or contact information from the stores; it can see basic order records (such as an order ID and which pack was bought) in the Apple and Google store dashboards. Store purchases and restoration require an internet connection, even though the core game is playable without one.

3. Sharing a score

If you choose to share a score, the iOS app opens the Share Sheet, and the Android app copies the text or link to your clipboard so you can paste it where you like. You decide whether to send it and which service receives it. Scores are not shared automatically. The recipient service's own privacy terms apply to anything you choose to send.

4. Duels and shared links

A duel works through a link that contains your run. When you choose to start or reply to a duel, the app builds a link that encodes what is needed to replay your run: the game rules version, the course seed, your score and hops, the playfield size (derived from your screen size), your aim-guide setting, the timing of your taps, the cosmetic look of your comet if you chose one, and a preset duel name. The duel name is picked once at random from a built-in list on your device; you do not type it, and it is not your real name. The link does not include an account, email address, device identifier or location.

This data sits in the part of the link after the "#" sign. Web browsers do not send that part to a website's server, and the duel page on this site does not upload it. The iOS app shares the link through the Share Sheet and the Android app copies it to your clipboard; either way, you decide whether to share a link and with whom. Anyone who has the link can open it, see the preset name and replay the run, and the link may be forwarded or be visible to the service you send it through (a messaging app, for example) under that service's own privacy terms. Please share links only with people you want to see them, and do not add personal information to any message you send with a link.

A friend plays the run by opening the link in a web browser, on this support website's duel page; the apps do not open duel links. The duel page is the full game running in the visitor's browser, and it saves a preset duel name, best score, progress, settings and tutorial flags in that browser's local storage.

5. Game Center (optional)

On iOS, if you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the app reports your scores to the Zipwaltz leaderboards and unlocks achievements through Game Center. Apple provides this service under its own terms and privacy practices, and your Game Center nickname and scores can be visible to other Game Center players. ShortyGames does not run a server that receives this information. You can play without Game Center and can manage it in your device's Settings.

6. Clips (iOS)

On iOS, the game can record a short clip of a run (about 10 seconds) using Apple's ReplayKit. The clip captures the game screen and game audio, not your microphone. It is kept as a temporary file on your device and only leaves the device if you choose to share it through the Share Sheet or save it to Photos (the app asks for permission to add to your photos). We do not upload clips.

7. Advertising, analytics and tracking

The iOS and Android games do not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and do not use an advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. This support website does not install ShortyGames analytics scripts or advertising cookies. If these practices change in a future release, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Apple and Google can separately process App Store purchases, downloads, diagnostics and aggregate usage information under their own settings and privacy practices. The fact that a device uses those services does not mean game scores are uploaded to ShortyGames.

8. Contacting support

When you contact slingshotplanet-support.tiling324@simplelogin.com, we receive the email address you use, the message content and any attachments you choose to include. We use this information to answer your request, investigate game defects and maintain related support records. Please do not send sensitive information such as passwords, verification codes or payment-card details.

Our public support alias is routed through SimpleLogin, and the private support inbox is hosted with Proton Mail. These providers process support messages as needed to deliver, protect and store email. We do not use support inquiries for targeted advertising or sell support correspondents' information. We retain support correspondence only as reasonably needed to provide support, resolve issues or comply with applicable record-keeping obligations. You can request deletion of a support conversation by contacting us, subject to any necessary legal or security retention.

9. Website hosting and external links

These pages are static and hosted using GitHub Pages. ShortyGames does not run a site visitor-account system, embedded contact form, advertising pixel or site analytics on these pages. GitHub may process technical request data, such as IP addresses, for website hosting, security and service operation according to its own privacy practices.

The duel page loads its two fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address and browser details when you open it, under Google's own privacy practices. The page itself sends no other data to ShortyGames or to any analytics service.

Links to Apple, Google Play, itch.io, email applications and other external services take you to independently operated services with their own privacy terms.

10. Your choices

  • You can play without creating a game account or buying any pack.
  • You may choose whether to share a score or a duel link, and whether to email support.
  • You may change or remove locally stored game data using your device's app and backup controls.
  • You may ask us about your support messages or request their deletion using the contact address below.
  • Apple Account and Google Play purchase-history and payment requests are handled through Apple's and Google's account and support channels.

11. Android app

Zipwaltz on Android (com.shortygames.zipwaltz) works the same way as the iOS game, apart from Apple-specific features: you play on your own device, with no account, no ads and no analytics or third-party SDKs other than Google Play Billing for the optional purchases in section 2. Scores, progress and settings are stored only on your device, and gameplay works offline.

The Android app requests internet access (and network-state access). The game itself makes no network requests; the permission is there for the Google Play Billing library, which uses it to buy and restore the optional purchases through the Google Play store on your device. Google Play Billing and the Google Play services components it brings may send purchase and diagnostic data to Google under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details or contact information from them; we can see basic order records (such as an order ID and which pack was bought) in the store dashboards. Section 5 (Game Center) applies only to iOS. If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Google may include the game's local data in your device backup under Google's own terms; you can turn this off in Android settings. Scores and duel links are never shared automatically. Uninstalling the app deletes its data from the device.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game's features, service providers or legal obligations change. The effective or updated date above will show when a revision applies.

For privacy questions or support requests, email slingshotplanet-support.tiling324@simplelogin.com.
